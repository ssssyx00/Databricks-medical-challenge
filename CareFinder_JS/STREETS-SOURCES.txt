Street detail and labels

© OpenStreetMap contributors. Street geometry and names from OpenFreeMap public OpenMapTiles-schema vector tiles. Snapshot 20261115_080022_pt, with online tiles used for additional detail.
https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://openfreemap.org/
https://openmaptiles.org/

The included streets-data.js extract is distributed under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. It contains compressed collections of roads, waterways, land cover, places and names, selected and generalized from the source tiles. It can be inspected with the unpacking/rendering code in streets.js. Tile style, symbols and label placement are customized for this application.
